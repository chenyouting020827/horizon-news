## [Black hole singularity is a surface, not a point](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21590) ⭐️ 6.0/10

A new arXiv paper (2608.21590) clarifies that the black hole singularity is a spacelike surface in spacetime, not a zero-dimensional point at the center. This corrects a common pop-science misconception about what lies inside a black hole. The paper helps correct widespread public misconceptions about black holes, emphasizing that even common visualizations oversimplify general relativity. It also highlights the subtle causal structure inside a black hole, which is important for physics education and science communication. In the Schwarzschild interior, the singularity behaves more like a future moment in time than a location in space, meaning infalling observers encounter it as an endpoint of their timeline. The paper also notes that two infalling observers do not meet at the singularity; they lose causal contact with each other long before reaching it.

hackernews · raattgift · Aug 25, 17:02 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49437210)

**Background**: In general relativity, a singularity is a point where spacetime curvature becomes infinite and the theory breaks down. For a non-rotating black hole, the Schwarzschild solution predicts a singularity that is spacelike, meaning it is not a point in space but a boundary in time. Pop-science accounts often inaccurately portray it as a point at the center of the hole, ignoring this distinction. The paper uses a Penrose diagram to illustrate the correct structure.
