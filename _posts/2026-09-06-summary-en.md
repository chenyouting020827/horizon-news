## [Nitter unarchived: privacy-friendly Twitter front-end to continue development](https://github.com/zedeus/nitter/commit/1428b4c2b4246f92a7e5b2673438e5fb39fcc4a3) ⭐️ 7.0/10

The Nitter project has been unarchived on GitHub, reversing its previous archived status and signaling that development will continue. The commit by project lead zedeus indicates an active path forward for the popular privacy-focused Twitter/X alternative. Nitter is one of the most popular privacy-focused alternative front-ends for Twitter/X, so its continued development matters to users who want to browse tweets without JavaScript, ads, or IP-based tracking. The reversal also resolves months of uncertainty within the open-source and privacy communities about whether the project would be abandoned. Nitter works by routing all requests through its own backend and using Twitter's unofficial API to scrape content, meaning the client never communicates directly with Twitter. It is roughly 15 times lighter than Twitter and can load timelines 2–4 times faster, making it a practical alternative for low-bandwidth or privacy-sensitive users.

hackernews · zImPatrick · Sep 6, 17:49 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49588988)

**Background**: Nitter is a free, open-source alternative Twitter/X front-end inspired by Invidious, a similar alternative front-end for YouTube. On GitHub, archiving a repository makes it read-only and signals that it is no longer actively maintained; unarchiving restores the ability to push changes, open issues, and continue development. Nitter's earlier archival had raised concerns about its future, so this unarchiving is a notable reversal.

## [NetBSD 9.5 Released; NetBSD 9 Branch Reaches End of Life](https://blog.netbsd.org/tnf/entry/netbsd_9_5_released_and) ⭐️ 5.0/10

The NetBSD project announced the release of NetBSD 9.5 and simultaneously declared the NetBSD 9 branch end-of-life. Existing NetBSD 9 users are being urged to upgrade to a currently supported release. This matters because NetBSD 9 will no longer receive maintenance or security fixes, leaving older deployments exposed unless they migrate. It signals a clear transition point for users who value NetBSD's portability but rely on the stability of an established series. NetBSD 9.5 is the final maintenance release of the NetBSD 9 series, and after this point no further updates or security advisories will be issued for the branch. Users seeking a supported release need to move to a newer version, while the project continues to support a wide range of hardware platforms.

hackernews · jaypatelani · Sep 6, 15:44 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49587636)

**Background**: NetBSD is a free, open-source Unix-like operating system known for high portability; it supports over 59 hardware platforms across 16 instruction set architectures. Its hardware abstraction layer separates machine-independent and machine-dependent driver components, making a single driver portable across many systems. In NetBSD's versioning model, a numbered series such as NetBSD 9 receives maintenance releases until it is declared end-of-life.
