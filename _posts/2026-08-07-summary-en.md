Taalas' team, including co-founder and former Tenstorrent CEO Ljubisa Bajic, will join AMD's AI organization under Vamsi Boppana. The AI models used were Evo1 and Evo2, genomic foundation models developed by researchers at Stanford University and the Arc Institute.

Generative AI models, such as Evo1 and Evo2, are large language models trained on genomic data that can generate novel DNA sequences. De novo genome synthesis enables scientists to physically build a genome from chemically synthesized DNA fragments. This work demonstrates that AI can generate complex, functional biological code, but it also raises biosecurity governance questions.

## [Humans Missed 1 in 3 Threats in AI Agent Permissions Game](https://scalex.dev/blog/ai-agent-permissions-stats/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A statistical analysis of a 40,000-run AI agent permission game revealed that human players missed 1 in 3 dangerous prompts, with over 409,000 decisions collected. The game, shared on Hacker News, found even with a warning upfront, users approved a significant number of malicious commands. This highlights a critical weakness in human oversight of AI agents, which is a key security concern as agentic AI becomes more widely deployed. The findings suggest that relying on users to approve AI commands is not a reliable safety mechanism, impacting how AI agent permissions should be designed. The game included a timer and a history log above npm run commands, which was typically ignored by players. Community feedback from a previous Hacker News thread was incorporated, including a point about npm run ambiguity, but many commenters still questioned the test's validity due to misleading prompts and zero real-world stakes.

hackernews · Wirbelwind · Aug 6, 11:58 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49195468)

**Background**: AI agents are software systems that use large language models (LLMs) to autonomously plan and execute tasks, often by issuing commands to tools and services. In many designs, each action requires the user to approve a permission prompt, which acts as a safety check. However, prompt injection is a cyberattack where malicious instructions are embedded in inputs that an AI agent processes, potentially leading to unintended or dangerous actions. This game simulated that scenario, testing whether humans could distinguish safe from malicious commands under time pressure.
